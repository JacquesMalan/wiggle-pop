A note from the maker

Built from real work-from-home days.

I’m a dad who works from home. School holidays have always been a challenge, especially now that my two daughters, aged four and five, want to be involved and “work with me”.

That is what inspired Wiggle Pop: from wiggling and tapping to popping the keyboard. When friends’ children enjoyed it just as much, I decided to share the experience with other families.

More parent tools and helpful software support may come later.
